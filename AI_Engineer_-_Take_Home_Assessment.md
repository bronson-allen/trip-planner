**Take-home Project (self-paced)** 

**3 Days in Italy** 

**The Brief** 

You have 3 days in Italy. The file `italy.json` contains \~100 places across several cities and regions — restaurants, historic sites, museums, vineyards, coastal towns, markets, and experiences. 

Build an interactive trip planner that helps a user create a personalized 3-day itinerary from this data. 

**The Data** 

Each entry in `italy.json` includes: 

\- Name, type, city/region, and neighborhood 

\- Opening hours and typical visit duration 

\- Price range and rating 

\- A short description 

\- Latitude and longitude 

\- Tags (e.g., "quiet", "scenic", "local favorite", "food", "historic", "outdoors") 

The data is realistic, not clean. Some places have seasonal hours, some have incomplete information, and not everything will be internally consistent. 

The provided dataset is your source of truth for places. You may enrich it with external services (maps, routing, images, etc.), if useful, but it is not required. You must use this dataset though. 

**What to Submit** 

1\. **A live URL** deployed somewhere we can visit and use. 

2\. **Your code** in a repo we can review, either on GitHub or zipped up.  
3\. **A short note**: either a written doc (1-2 pages max) or a short video walkthrough (5 min max), covering: 

\- What you built and why 

\- What you'd do with more time 

\- How you used AI during this project — as a building tool, as a product feature, both, or neither 

**Guidelines** 

\- **Tech stack:** Pick a web framework you're most familiar with (e.g. React, Next.js) \- **AI:** Use any tools you'd normally use when building. We care about the end result and your decisions, not purity of process. 

\- **Time:** There's no hard timebox. We expect most candidates will spend a few hours on this. 

\- There are no hidden requirements. We're looking at what you choose to build, how well you execute it, and the judgment calls you make along the way.
